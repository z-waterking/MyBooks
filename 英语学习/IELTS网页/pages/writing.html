通用 4 段结构

i.

引言 (40–50 词)

1. 改写题目背景（用同义替换，不要直接抄题）。
2. 表明本文将讨论 / 你的立场。

ii.

主体 1 (90–100 词)

1. 主题句（中心论点）。
2. 解释 / 举例 / 因果。
3. 小结一句。

iii.

主体 2 (90–100 词)

同主体 1 结构，提出第二个论点（或反方论点）。

iv.

结论 (40–50 词)

1. 重申立场（不要引入新观点）。
2. 概括两个主体的要点。

§ III · MODEL ESSAYS

5 篇完整范文 · 一种题型一篇

TYPE A · AGREE / DISAGREE

范文 1 · 健康税

Prompt: Some people believe that the best way to improve public health is to increase the price of unhealthy foods. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

Introduction (49 words)

It is often argued that imposing higher prices on unhealthy foods would meaningfully reduce diet-related disease. While I partly agree that such measures may discourage casual consumption, I believe that education, rather than taxation, is the more sustainable means of improving public health.

Body 1 — Acknowledging the partial validity of price (95 words)

Admittedly, higher prices can shift behaviour, at least at the margins. Mexico's 2014 tax on sugary drinks, for example, led to a 7.6% reduction in their consumption within two years, according to public health data. When consumers feel a financial pinch, particularly for non-essential items like soft drinks and crisps, they often switch to cheaper, healthier alternatives. In this sense, price signals can be a useful component of a broader strategy.

Body 2 — Why education is the better strategy (102 words)

However, taxation alone tends to be both regressive and short-lived in its effect. It disproportionately burdens lower-income households, who already spend a larger share of income on food, and does little to address the underlying ignorance about nutrition. Programmes such as the UK's "Change4Life", which combine school workshops with practical cooking lessons, have produced more durable shifts in family eating habits than price increases alone. Once people understand why a balanced diet matters, their choices change without needing economic coercion.

Conclusion (45 words)

In conclusion, although taxing unhealthy foods may produce modest short-term gains, I maintain that education provides a more equitable and lasting path to a healthier population. The two approaches should be complementary, but if forced to prioritise, governments should invest in informing citizens before charging them.

本范文学习要点

  • 立场用 "partly agree"（部分同意）—— 思辨感强，比纯同意/反对易得分。
  • 具体数据 + 真实政策名（Mexico 2014, Change4Life）—— Task Achievement 分数关键。
  • 承让步：admittedly / however / although — 体现立场层次。
  • 词汇替换：unhealthy foods → diet-related disease / non-essential items / junk food avoided.
  • 共 291 词，符合要求。
TYPE B · ADV / DIS

范文 2 · 居家办公

Prompt: An increasing number of people now work from home rather than in a traditional office. Do the advantages of this trend outweigh the disadvantages?

In recent years, remote working has shifted from a rare perk to a mainstream arrangement for many professionals. In my view, although it carries some genuine drawbacks, the advantages clearly outweigh them, provided that employers take steps to mitigate the downsides.

One clear advantage is that home working eliminates the daily commute, which in major cities can easily consume two hours. This recovered time can be redirected towards family, exercise or rest — all of which contribute to long-term productivity and wellbeing. A 2015 Stanford University study found that employees who worked from home were on average 13% more productive than their office-based counterparts, partly because the home environment is less prone to interruption. For employers, the savings on office rent and utilities can be considerable.

That said, this arrangement is not without its drawbacks. The most obvious is the blurring of boundaries between work and personal life. Without the physical separation of "going to" and "leaving" an office, many remote workers find themselves answering emails at midnight, leading to burnout. Equally, the absence of casual office interactions weakens team cohesion and can isolate newer employees, who learn workplace norms largely through observation.

Weighing both sides, the productivity, financial and lifestyle gains appear to outweigh the social drawbacks, provided that companies introduce clear policies — such as fixed "no email" hours and quarterly in-person meet-ups — to protect workers' boundaries and team bonds. In conclusion, while home working is not without challenges, its benefits, properly managed, far exceed its costs.

本范文学习要点

  • 引言点明立场 + 加条件（"provided that..."）—— 让立场更精细。
  • 主体段中加入具体数据（13%, Stanford 2015）。
  • 用 "not without its drawbacks" 替代 "have drawbacks"，更高级。
  • 结尾再次提出具体解决方案 (fixed "no email" hours)，避免空话。
  • 共 291 词。
TYPE C · DISCUSS BOTH VIEWS

范文 3 · 艺术补贴

Prompt: Some people think that governments should give financial support to creative artists. Others believe that artists should earn their own money from the commercial market. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

Many countries have a long tradition of subsidising the arts, while others argue that artists should rely on commercial success. In my view, a balanced approach is most reasonable: state support should remain for projects of clear cultural significance, but artists working in mainstream fields should be expected to be self-sufficient.

Those who advocate public funding often point out that great art is rarely commercially viable in its own time. Van Gogh, for example, sold only one painting during his lifetime, yet his work now defines a major art movement. Without grants, fellowships or subsidised studios, many emerging or experimental artists would simply not survive long enough to find an audience. Moreover, certain forms — classical music, contemporary dance, regional theatre — serve a national cultural function and would disappear entirely without public backing.

On the other hand, those who oppose public funding argue that taxpayer money should not subsidise individual creative pursuits when many essential services remain underfunded. They contend that genuine demand should determine an artist's survival; if no one is willing to pay for a work, perhaps it does not deserve to exist on public funds. Commercial artists — pop musicians, novelists, filmmakers — operate this way and produce work that millions enjoy.

Weighing both views, I am inclined to support targeted public funding for non-commercial art forms with clear cultural value, while leaving commercially viable artists to the market. This protects cultural heritage without burdening taxpayers with the cost of every aspiring artist.

关键句式

  • "Those who advocate ... often point out that ..." — 论证对方立场
  • "On the other hand, those who oppose ... argue that ..." — 平衡呈现
  • "Weighing both views, I am inclined to ..." — 明确立场结尾
TYPE D · CAUSE + SOLUTION

范文 4 · 金融素养

Prompt: Many young people leave school with little knowledge of how to manage their money. What causes this, and how can the problem be addressed?

It is widely acknowledged that young adults today often struggle with basic financial decisions — from understanding interest rates to budgeting a monthly salary. This essay will examine the underlying causes and propose practical remedies.

The root cause lies in the rigid focus of modern curricula on subjects deemed academically rigorous, such as mathematics or science. Personal finance — arguably more useful in adult life than calculus — is dismissed as a 'soft' or 'optional' skill. As a result, students graduate able to solve abstract equations but unable to read a payslip or compare loan terms. A second factor is the reluctance of parents to discuss financial matters openly; in many cultures, money remains a taboo topic at the dinner table, depriving young people of basic exposure to budgeting and saving habits.

Several measures can address this issue. First and foremost, schools should integrate financial literacy into the compulsory curriculum from age 14, covering budgeting, taxation, debt, and the principles of investing. Some countries — notably Finland and the UK — have already introduced such modules, with measurable improvements in young adults' saving rates. Second, parents and community organisations could host workshops aimed specifically at school-leavers, normalising open conversations about money. Finally, banks and fintech companies should offer free, gamified apps to help young users practise these skills in low-stakes environments.

In conclusion, while the financial illiteracy of school-leavers stems from gaps in both formal education and family culture, a combined response from schools, parents and financial institutions can meaningfully equip the next generation to manage their money wisely.

TYPE E · TWO-PART QUESTION

范文 5 · 老龄化

Prompt: In many countries, the proportion of older people is steadily rising. Why is this happening? Does this trend have more positive or negative effects on society?

The age structure of populations across the developed world is shifting dramatically, with older citizens forming an ever larger share. Although this trend has clear downsides for public finances, on balance I believe its effects are more positive than negative.

The causes are well documented. First, life expectancy has risen sharply thanks to advances in medicine, public sanitation and nutrition; a child born in the UK today can expect to live some twenty years longer than one born a century ago. Second, birth rates have fallen, particularly in countries where women have gained access to higher education and careers. The combination produces a population that grows older on average each year.

The negative consequences are real. Pension systems, designed when retirement was brief, now face decades of payments per worker. Healthcare costs rise sharply for the over-70s. And in some economies, a shrinking workforce raises concerns about long-term productivity. Japan, where nearly 30% of citizens are over 65, has felt these pressures most acutely.

Yet the benefits are substantial. An ageing population is, by definition, evidence of progress: people living longer, healthier lives. Retired citizens contribute considerable unpaid value through grandchild care, community volunteering and lifelong learning. Older workers, too, often bring experience and stability that younger employees lack.

In conclusion, while ageing populations present fiscal challenges, the underlying reason — better, longer lives — is a triumph of modern society. With sensible reforms to pensions and healthcare, the gains far outweigh the losses.

§ VII · TASK 1 MODELS

Task 1 三种典型范文

LINE GRAPH

范文 · 线图 · 三国能源消耗

Prompt: The line graph below shows the consumption of electricity (in TWh) by three countries — A, B and C — between 2000 and 2020.

The line graph illustrates the electricity consumption, measured in terawatt-hours, of three countries (A, B and C) over a twenty-year period from 2000 to 2020.

Overall, while Country A saw a sustained and substantial increase across the period, Country B's consumption remained relatively stable, and Country C experienced a marked decline after 2010.

In 2000, Country A consumed approximately 200 TWh — the lowest of the three. Its electricity use then rose steadily, climbing past Country B around 2008 and reaching around 480 TWh by 2020, more than double its starting figure. Country B, by contrast, began the period as the highest consumer at 350 TWh; its consumption fluctuated mildly between 340 and 360 TWh before settling at 345 TWh in 2020 — essentially unchanged.

Country C's pattern stands in sharp contrast. From a peak of around 320 TWh in 2010, its consumption fell sharply, dropping to 220 TWh by 2020 — its lowest point across the period. This decline coincided with Country C's well-publicised investment in renewable infrastructure.

PIE CHART

范文 · 饼图 · 家庭支出

Prompt: The two pie charts compare household spending in a Western European country in 1990 and 2020.

The two pie charts compare the breakdown of household spending in a Western European country in 1990 and 2020.

Overall, the most striking shift over the thirty years is the significant rise in housing costs and the corresponding decline in food expenditure, while other categories saw more modest changes.

In 1990, food was the single largest expense, accounting for 30% of total household spending, followed by housing at 22% and transport at 15%. The remaining third was split between leisure (12%), education (10%) and miscellaneous (11%).

By 2020, housing had become the dominant category, representing 35% of spending — a rise of 13 percentage points. Food, in contrast, had fallen to just 18%, suggesting either improvements in food affordability or a real-terms increase in household incomes. Transport remained broadly stable at 14%, while leisure spending edged up to 14%. Education saw a slight rise to 12%, and miscellaneous expenditure dropped to 7%.

MAP

范文 · 地图 · 公园翻新

Prompt: The two maps show a town park in 2000 and after recent redevelopment in 2020.

The two maps depict the transformation of a town park between 2000 and 2020 following a major redevelopment.

Overall, the park has been significantly modernised, with the addition of recreational facilities and the removal of older, less-used features, although the central lake and main pathways have been retained.

In 2000, the park was dominated by a large lake at its centre, surrounded by extensive flower gardens to the north and a small playground to the south-east. A car park occupied the south-west corner, while the eastern boundary featured a row of tennis courts. Two paths crossed the park, intersecting near the lake.

By 2020, several substantial changes had taken place. The flower gardens to the north had been replaced by a community café and an outdoor exercise area. The old playground was expanded considerably and now included a water feature. The tennis courts were demolished to make way for a multi-purpose sports field, and the car park was reduced in size to accommodate a new bicycle parking area. The lake and main pathways, however, remained intact.

§ VIII · SELF-CHECK

写完后的 10 项自查

  1. 引言是否改写而不是抄题？
  2. 有没有清晰立场 (Task 2) / Overview (Task 1)？
  3. 每段有没有主题句？
  4. 有没有具体的例子或数据支撑论点？
  5. 词汇有没有重复使用 3 次以上？把它换掉。
  6. 句式有没有混用简单 + 复合 + 复杂？
  7. 字数够吗？(Task 1 ≥ 150, Task 2 ≥ 250)
  8. 有没有第一人称错用？(Task 1 不用 I think; Task 2 可用)
  9. 动词时态是否一致？(Task 1 历史数据用过去时；预测用将来时)
  10. 结论是否引入了新观点？(应只重申，不引新)
