# IELTS Speaking · 2026 高频话题预测（参考用）

> 雅思口语题库每 4 个月（1月、5月、9月）换一次。以下是基于近两个考期的高频 Part 2 卡片话题，建议各类别准备 1–2 个素材，做到「换题不换稿」。

---

## Part 1 高频话题（10 个万能模块）

### 1. Work / Studies
- What do you do? / What's your major?
- Do you like your job/major?
- Is it easy to find a job in your field?
- What's the most interesting part?

### 2. Hometown
- Where do you come from?
- Has it changed much in recent years?
- Is it a good place for young people / for tourists?

### 3. Accommodation
- Do you live in a house or a flat?
- Which is your favourite room?
- Would you like to change anything?

### 4. Food & Cooking
- What's your favourite food?
- Do you cook? How often?
- Have your eating habits changed?

### 5. Hobbies & Free time
- What do you usually do at weekends?
- Do you have a hobby?
- How have your hobbies changed over time?

### 6. Reading
- Do you enjoy reading?
- What kind of books do you read?
- Paper books or e-books?

### 7. Music
- Do you listen to music every day?
- What kind of music do you like?
- Do you play any instrument?

### 8. Travel
- Do you like travelling?
- What's the best trip you've taken?
- Do you prefer travelling alone or with others?

### 9. Sports
- Do you play any sports?
- Did you do sports when you were younger?
- Should children be encouraged to do more sport?

### 10. Weather
- What's the weather like in your hometown?
- Which season do you like best?
- Does weather affect your mood?

## Part 2 高频卡片（按类别）

### 人物类 (5 题)
- Describe a person you admire.
- Describe a teacher who has influenced you.
- Describe a creative person.
- Describe a leader you respect.
- Describe a friend who is a good leader.

### 地点类 (5 题)
- Describe a place you'd like to visit in the future.
- Describe a quiet place you like to go.
- Describe a city you would like to visit.
- Describe a building you find interesting.
- Describe a place where you can relax.

### 物品类 (5 题)
- Describe a gift you received.
- Describe something you bought online.
- Describe a useful app on your phone.
- Describe a piece of clothing you like.
- Describe a photo you took that you are proud of.

### 事件类 (5 题)
- Describe a memorable celebration.
- Describe a time when you were really busy.
- Describe an occasion when you helped someone.
- Describe an important decision you made.
- Describe a time when you learned something new.

### 媒体类 (4 题)
- Describe a movie you would recommend.
- Describe a book that changed your view.
- Describe a song that means a lot to you.
- Describe a podcast you enjoy.

### 经历类 (5 题)
- Describe a difficult task you completed.
- Describe a journey you took by public transport.
- Describe a skill you learned recently.
- Describe a time you got lost in a place.
- Describe a time you tried something for the first time.

## Part 3 抽象问题样例

> P3 由 P2 延伸而来，话题更抽象。以下是常见 P3 提问角度：

**关于"科技"**：
- Do you think technology has made people lazier?
- Is technology changing the way we communicate?
- Will technology replace human teachers?

**关于"教育"**：
- Is the role of schools changing?
- Should children learn life skills in school?
- Is online learning as effective as classroom learning?

**关于"工作"**：
- Why do people change jobs more often nowadays?
- Is work-life balance more important than salary?
- Will artificial intelligence replace many jobs?

**关于"环境"**：
- What can individuals do to protect the environment?
- Whose responsibility is environmental protection?
- Are large companies doing enough?

**关于"传统 / 现代化"**：
- Are traditional festivals losing their meaning?
- Should governments preserve old buildings?
- How can we balance progress and tradition?

## 万能"故事素材"（覆盖 80% 卡片）

建议提前准备 4 段你自己的"母素材"，做到换题不换稿：

1. **大学期间一次重要的小组合作 / 项目** —— 可对应：difficult task / helped someone / learning experience / leadership / creative person
2. **一次给亲友的特别礼物 / 收到的特别礼物** —— 可对应：gift / object / event / person you care about
3. **一次记忆深刻的旅行** —— 可对应：journey / place / getting lost / outdoor activity / memorable event
4. **一本/一部对你影响深的书 / 电影** —— 可对应：media / influence / experience that changed your view

---

## 提分建议

- 不要背稿；考官识别背诵的能力远超你想象。
- 每个 P2 准备 5–6 个关键词，能即兴展开即可。
- 录音自练：手机录 2 分钟回答 → 回听 → 找填充词 / 长停顿 / 重复用词。
- P3 多用 "I'd say..." / "On the one hand..." / "If we compare..." 等 hedging 句式，思考时间更自然。
