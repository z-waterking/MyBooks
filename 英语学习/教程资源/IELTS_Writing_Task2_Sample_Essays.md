## 题型 A · Agree / Disagree

**题目**：*Some people think that governments should give financial support to creative artists such as painters and musicians. Others believe that artists should earn their own money in the commercial market. Discuss both views and give your opinion.* (Cambridge IELTS 12, Test 2)

### 范文片段（Introduction + Body 1）

> Many countries have a long tradition of supporting the arts through public funding, while others argue that artists should rely on private patrons or commercial success. In my view, **a balanced approach is most reasonable**: state support should be available for projects of cultural significance, but artists working in commercial fields should be expected to be self-sufficient.
>
> Those who advocate public funding often point out that great art is rarely commercially viable in its own time. **Van Gogh, for example**, sold only one painting during his lifetime, yet his work is now considered foundational to modern art. Without some form of support — whether grants, fellowships, or subsidised studio space — many emerging or experimental artists would simply not survive. **In addition**, certain art forms such as classical music or contemporary theatre often serve a national cultural function and would disappear without public backing.

**学习要点**：
- 改写题目：用 "have a long tradition of..." 替换 "should support..."
- 立场词：a balanced approach
- 论据引入：those who advocate... often point out that...
- 举例：Van Gogh, for example
- 连接：In addition / Without... / Whether...

## 题型 B · Advantages outweigh Disadvantages

**题目**：*Many people work in their homes via computers and telephones. Some people say this is good for workers and employers. Others say it may have a negative effect. Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?*

### 范文片段（Body）

> One clear advantage of home working is that it eliminates the daily commute, which in major cities can take up two hours or more. This saved time can be redirected towards family, exercise or rest — all of which contribute to long-term productivity. **A study by Stanford University** found that employees who worked from home were on average **13% more productive** than their office-based counterparts.
>
> **Nevertheless**, this arrangement is not without its drawbacks. The most obvious is **the blurring of boundaries between work and personal life**. Without the physical separation of "going to" and "leaving" an office, many remote workers report difficulties switching off in the evenings, leading to higher rates of burnout. Furthermore, the **absence of casual office interactions** can isolate workers and weaken team cohesion.
>
> **Weighing both sides**, the productivity and lifestyle gains appear to outweigh the social drawbacks, **provided that** companies introduce clear policies to protect workers' time.

**学习要点**：
- 让步—转折结构：Nevertheless / However
- 数据增强说服力：A study by... found that...
- 名词化：the blurring of boundaries / the absence of casual interactions
- 收尾：Weighing both sides, ... provided that...

## 题型 C · Cause + Solution

**题目**：*In many countries, the proportion of older people is steadily increasing. Does this trend have more positive or negative effects on society?*

### 范文片段（Body 2 — Solutions）

> A range of policies can mitigate these challenges. **Firstly**, governments should reform pension systems so that they remain financially sustainable as life expectancy rises; some Nordic countries have already done this by gradually raising the retirement age. **Secondly**, encouraging older citizens to remain economically active — through part-time roles, mentoring programmes or volunteer work — can both ease the labour shortage and contribute to their sense of purpose. **Finally**, investment in age-friendly infrastructure (accessible public transport, community health centres, social hubs) helps the elderly stay connected and reduces the long-term burden on hospitals.

**学习要点**：
- 列举：Firstly / Secondly / Finally（也可换 First and foremost / Equally important / Last but not least）
- 平行结构：reforming X / encouraging Y / investing in Z
- 双重收益句式：can both... and...
- 范围词：a range of / a variety of

## 题型 D · Two-part question

**题目**：*Many young people leave school without good knowledge of how to manage their money. Why is this so? What can be done to address this problem?*

### 范文片段（Body 1)

> The root cause **lies in the rigid focus of modern curricula** on subjects deemed academically rigorous, such as mathematics or science. Personal finance, despite being arguably more useful in adult life than calculus, is often dismissed as a 'soft' or 'optional' skill. **As a result**, students graduate able to solve abstract equations but unable to read a payslip or compare loan terms. A second factor is the **reluctance of parents** to discuss financial matters openly with their children; in many cultures, money is considered a taboo topic at the dinner table, depriving young people of even the most basic exposure to budgeting.

**学习要点**：
- 因果句式：The root cause lies in... / A second factor is...
- 矛盾对比：able to solve X but unable to do Y
- 抽象→具体：personal finance → read a payslip / compare loan terms

## 通用万能句式库

### 引言改写句式
- It is often argued that... / It has long been a matter of debate whether...
- In recent years, an increasing number of people have started to...
- The question of whether... is a contentious one.

### 表明立场
- Personally, I am inclined to agree with the latter view.
- While I acknowledge the merits of..., I believe that...
- Although both sides have valid points, I am convinced that...

### 引入论点
- One of the strongest arguments in favour of... is that...
- A common objection to this view is that...
- It is important to consider that...

### 举例 / 数据
- Take... as an example.
- A case in point is...
- According to a recent study by..., ...
- For instance,...

### 让步与反驳
- Admittedly, ..., but...
- While it is true that..., this does not mean that...
- Critics may argue that..., however,...

### 推理 / 因果
- This leads to a situation in which...
- Consequently, / As a result, / Thereby...
- The most obvious consequence is that...

### 收尾
- In conclusion, while X has its place, Y remains the more effective approach.
- All things considered, the benefits clearly outweigh the costs.
- To sum up, both sides of this debate raise legitimate concerns; however, ...
